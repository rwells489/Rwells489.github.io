Bio

Who I am

I started in IT in 1997 — system administration, user support, infrastructure — and earned a BS in Management Information Systems along the way. Then I spent two decades building and running a successful real estate business: finding clients, negotiating contracts, managing finance and compliance, and staying in business through every market the 2000s could throw at me.

In 2024 I came back to technology deliberately. I enrolled in the AWS Cloud Institute's Cloud Application Developer program, earned my AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner, and — because certifications alone don't prove much — built a production-grade platform of my own: a self-hosted AI agent system running on AWS EC2 behind a zero-inbound Tailscale network, with its own dashboard, vector database, orchestration layer, and security model.

What I bring that a typical junior cloud developer can't: I've owned a P&L. I know what it costs when systems fail, what documentation is worth, and how to talk to the people paying for the infrastructure. I treat every build like a business, because for twenty years, it was.
